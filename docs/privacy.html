Fidelity Ledger Companion privacy policy

This independently developed extension helps you save your Fidelity portfolio records for use with the Fidelity Ledger application on your own computer. It is not affiliated with Fidelity. Published by Dan Argue. Contact: danargue@hey.com. Effective date: October 24, 2026.

Information handled

While you are signed into Fidelity, the extension processes account identifiers, positions, balances, transactions, pending orders and requested statements. It also stores capture progress and download receipts locally. The automatic-download preference uses Chrome Sync and may sync through Google if you enable browser sync; portfolio records are never stored in Chrome Sync. It does not ask for your Fidelity password or MFA code, or export authentication cookies. Fidelity itself receives your normal authenticated website requests.

Where information goes

Exports are saved through Chrome to your computer. Supported exports are sent to the local ledger at 127.0.0.1:8765, a loopback address on that same computer. The publisher does not receive your portfolio records. There is no advertising or analytics SDK, sale of personal information, or collection of unrelated browsing. Chrome's extension distribution/update services are operated by Google separately.

Controls and retention

Automatic capture is off for a new installation until you enable it in the popup. Manual download buttons start the capture you request. Turn automatic capture off in the extension popup or remove the extension to stop future captures. Removing the extension does not delete files you downloaded or records already imported into the local application. The ledger retains imported source evidence and its database until you remove or restore those local files. The extension may delete superseded downloads only after the local ledger confirms those exact files were archived. Local backups are under your control.

Limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide the prominently described portfolio export and local ledger functions.

Support

For support or privacy questions, email danargue@hey.com. Do not send passwords, MFA codes or raw financial exports when requesting help; start with an error message and app/extension versions. Any sharing of diagnostic files is optional and should be reviewed for personal information first.
