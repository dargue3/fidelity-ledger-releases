Ledger Companion

Independent portfolio recordkeeping for your Mac. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any brokerage.

Save positions, pending orders, account activity and statements from your authenticated brokerage website. Keep the records on your computer and hand supported exports to your local ledger application.

Choose manual capture or opt in to automatic downloads. Login and multi-factor authentication remain on your brokerage website. No trading, advertising, or publisher access to your financial records.

The companion requires the compatible local ledger app for importing and viewing your records. For setup help, contact the publisher.
